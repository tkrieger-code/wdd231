Membership Levels & Benefits

NP Membership

For non-profit organizations seeking community engagement.

Essential benefits for small businesses and startups.

Maximum visibility, spotlight features, and premium benefits.

NP Membership Benefits

  • Free membership for registered Non-Profits.
  • Directory listing on the Chamber website.
  • Access to general networking events.

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • Includes all NP benefits.
  • Discounted admission to Chamber workshops and seminars.
  • Member news published in monthly newsletters.

Silver Membership Benefits

  • Includes all Bronze benefits.
  • Homepage Spotlight rotation eligibility.
  • Free access to bi-monthly networking breakfasts.
  • 15% discount on event sponsorships.

Gold Membership Benefits

  • Includes all Silver benefits.
  • Priority Homepage Spotlight placement.
  • VIP seating and recognition at annual Chamber galas.
  • Free booth space at Chamber trade expos.
  • Dedicated email blast to all Chamber members once per year.
